# Independent confirmation, first attempt: device trials vs regulatory clearances

Generated by `write_report.py` from `lag_analysis.json`; no number is typed by hand. Analysis pre-declared in DECISIONS D-022 **before** the association was computed; collection described in D-022 and `collect.log`.

## Why this exists

Cycle 4 measured that the shipped data spine contains no source able to confirm a detected regulatory change: the only other registry present (ClinicalTrials.gov) is mapped to drug tracks, disjoint from the device tracks (0 of 104 cells). B-040 closed that gap by collecting device trials into a **new, disposable** database, leaving the frozen research lane untouched.

## What the new lane is

* ClinicalTrials.gov API v2, interventional studies, window matching the detection lane (2016-09 … 2026-10); knowledge time = study first-posted date.
* Sponsored by the curated 51-company device watchlist; sponsor resolution and device-track mapping reuse the repository's audited collector rather than a parallel implementation.
* Bounded run: 82 sponsor terms, 107 requests, 0 errors, no truncation reported by the collector's own caps.
* 4,130 studies returned → 2,663 interventional studies in window → **2,663 trial events**; 1,472 (55 %) attributable to a curated company, 1,278 (48 %) mapped to a device track, 1,181 device-intervention studies with a device track, 43 distinct device tracks.

## The test

Cells are (curated company, device track) pairs carrying both lanes, subject to pre-registered floors of ≥6 regulatory events and ≥3 trial events.

* 26 cells exist in both lanes; **16 pass the floors** (579 regulatory + 199 trial events).
* Statistic: cross-correlation at lags -6 … 12 months (positive lag = regulatory activity leads trial activity).
* Pooled by Fisher z; null distribution from circularly shifting each cell's trial series (2000 shifts, seed 7), which preserves every series' own autocorrelation and volume and destroys only the cross-series alignment.

## What this licenses, and what it does not

**Licensed.** At the (company, device track) granularity these two independent lanes carry **no detectable aligned time structure** in either direction between 6 months before and 12 months after a regulatory event. Any claim that device-trial activity and clearance activity confirm one another at cell level is unsupported by this test, including the "evidence fusion" claim that motivated collecting the lane.

**Not licensed.** This is *not* evidence that trials and clearances are unrelated. Three limits dominate:

* **Coverage.** 26 cells exist in both lanes and only 16 clear the activity floors. Sponsor resolution covers 55 % of returned studies and device-track mapping 48 %; the lane is a bounded sample, not a census.
* **Sparsity.** Per-cell series are dominated by zero months (the floors are 6 and 3 events over 119 months), so a per-cell correlation is mostly a co-occurrence pattern of rare months rather than a rate relationship.
* **Granularity.** Trials attach to a company, not to a legal sub-entity, so a company with many subsidiaries may have trials and clearances recorded at different entities and still be pooled into one cell; conversely, the track mapping of a trial is keyword-based.

## Consequence for the programme

The independent-confirmation question is now **answerable in principle but unanswerable in practice with this sample**: the lane exists and is committed, the test is pre-registered and re-runnable, and the answer at this granularity is null. Getting a real answer requires either (a) much denser independent evidence per cell — hiring and patents are the two declared gaps — or (b) a different unit of analysis, such as company-level entry cohorts rather than cell-level monthly counts. Both are backlog items, not conclusions.
