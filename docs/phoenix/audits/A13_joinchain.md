## 1) What it is and who buys it

Joinchain is the **longest-established Chinese device-industry data business** in this set: it built its platform in 2019 (claiming the first Chinese device big-data platform) and launched 医械数据云 / MDCLOUD in 2020. It is unusual among the seven in that it is **vertically deep into the Chinese regulatory and industrial-policy universe** (parks, provincial approvals, 招商, innovation-approval catalogues) rather than into global commercial intelligence.

Its buyers are split in a way no other platform here exhibits:
- **Device companies** — investment (投资), R&D (研发), market (市场), sales (销售) — the same four scenarios as NextDevice.
- **Industry parks, local governments and 招商 (investment-attraction) agencies** — evidenced by park databases, 集聚区/主题园区 fields, and "优质企业/上市企业/兴盛企业" targeting features.
- **Data buyers via data exchanges** — the 48–50 listed data products constitute a distinct B2B channel with formal compliance assessment.

Notably it is the only platform here with a **government/园区 customer segment** and the only one whose data is a **listed tradable asset**.

## 3) Data structure & standardisation principles

The richest *dataset inventory* of the seven, organised as a federated set of named databases linked by keys (E7):

| Layer | Databases evidenced |
|---|---|
| **Product** | 产品分类库, 产品信息库, 进口产品库, 创新优先审批库, 临床试验豁免目录 |
| **Organisation** | 生产企业库, 经营企业库, 委托生产备案, 园区库, 服务机构 |
| **Commercial** | 医用耗材集中采购库, 招投标标讯数据, 中国海关数据, 中国海关商品编码目录, 医保目录, 国产优秀设备 |
| **Knowledge** | 行业标准库, 政策法规, 临床文献, 流行病学, 不良事件, 产品召回, 知识产权 |
| **Geo/industry** | 省市, 集聚区/主题园区, 产业宏观 |

The standardisation spine is **classification-led**: a 产品分类库 (product classification database) is the hub that other databases are joined to (E7). Two published taxonomy depths:
- **三级分类** — a three-level product classification (E18), claimed as China's first for registered device products.
- **四级赛道分类** — a four-level "track" (sector) classification, with 标准赛道 (standard tracks) and 特色赛道 (custom/distinctive tracks) as separate flavours (E12).

That two-taxonomy design — a *regulatory/product* hierarchy (3 levels) plus a *market/track* hierarchy (4 levels) — is the single most developed taxonomy architecture found in this audit, and it directly parallels the MedRadar problem of needing both a technology classification and a market segmentation.

The customs linkage (中国海关商品编码目录, E10) is architecturally notable: it maps device products to **HS customs codes**, giving an import/export trade dimension that no other platform here exposes.

## 4) Company identity resolution

Documented at the **attribute** level, not the merge level:

- The 生产企业库 (manufacturer database) key fields are **企业名称**, 注册时间, 注册资金, 企业所属省市区, 集聚区/主题园区, 科技型中小企业/高新技术企业 flags, and **企业分类** (enterprise classification) — E5.
- Distinct *roles* are modelled as separate databases: 生产企业 (manufacturer), 经营企业 (distributor/operator), **委托生产备案** (contract-manufacturing filings), 代理 product chains.
- 企业分类 (E5) implies a firm-type taxonomy (likely 高新/科技型/上市/规上), i.e. **classification of companies**, not **resolution of company identities**.

What is **not** documented: any statement about subsidiaries, parent groups, M&A, rebrands, or Chinese↔English name pairing. There is no named master-entity ID equivalent to GBI's "OneID". "分类归整" (E15, classify-and-organise) is the closest phrase, and it is a *sorting* action, not an *identity reconciliation* action.

**Verdict:** role-aware and attribute-rich company modelling (VERIFIED); corporate-hierarchy identity resolution **UNKNOWN and apparently not a marketed capability**. For a platform whose original product was a manufacturer directory, this is the weakest dimension.

## 5) Technology / product taxonomy

The strongest published taxonomy architecture in this set:

- **产品分类 (3-level)** — E18. Three levels of product classification for registered devices. Node lists not public.
- **赛道 (4-level)** — E12: four-level track classification, split into 标准赛道 and 特色赛道. Node lists not public.
- **Regulatory class** — Ⅰ/Ⅱ/Ⅲ 类 via 生产范围 (E8) and registration records.
- **Domain sections** (E13, E15): 10 database 版块 and a 9-section clinical-R&D structure, which function as top-level topic facets.

Depth: **3 levels product / 4 levels sector** is the deepest *published* claim of any platform here (NextDevice and DevicePro publish dimensions but not depth; Clarivate publishes neither). But: no node is inspectable without a subscription, so depth is CLAIMED_UNVERIFIED in substance even though the level count is VERIFIED as a statement.

No technology-generation (技术代际) concept appears anywhere — the notable contrast with NextDevice.

## 6) Data provenance & evidence presentation

The most formal provenance position of the seven, because data-exchange listing forces disclosure:

- **Sources named by agency**: 国家药监局, 全国各省药监局, 各市场监督管理局 (E4) — i.e. NMPA + provincial + municipal regulators explicitly.
- **Compliance assessment across four dimensions** (E11): 主体信息 (vendor entity), 数据来源 (data source), 可交易性 (tradability), 流通风险 (circulation risk). This is an externally-reviewed provenance statement — the only one in this audit.
- **Update cadence published**: 月度更新 for the manufacturer database (E6).
- **Human-in-the-loop stated**: "人工二次复核" (E16) on tender cleaning.

Not evidenced: per-record source URL, document deep-links, per-record as-of dates, or version history. "可追溯" is not used as a headline word the way GBI uses it.

**Verdict:** strongest **institutional** provenance (exchange compliance + named agencies + cadence); still no **record-level** citation visible.

## 7) Competitor comparison features

Evidenced comparison models (E12, E16):
- **市场排名分析模型** — market-ranking analysis model.
- **价格区间分析模型** — price-band analysis model.
- **进口替代** (import substitution) analysis — a distinctively Chinese competitive frame: domestic vs imported share shift.
- **企业聚类** (company clustering) and **省市获批统计** (approvals by province/city) — geographic competitive structure.
- MDBIDS: 商机线索 / 采购单位 / 渠道企业 / 生产企业 six modules — i.e. comparison across **lead, buyer, channel, manufacturer** roles.
- MDRADAR: 新建医院 (new hospital builds), 招采动态, 甲方分析 (buyer-side analysis) — demand-side competitive signals.

So Joinchain's comparison frame is **price band + market rank + import-substitution + geography + channel role**. It is the most *commercially operational* comparison model of the seven for China, and notably includes the **buyer** (医院/采购单位) as a first-class competitive object — which no other platform here does.

## 8) Technology-generation expression

**Absent.** No 技术代际, no generation, no iteration tracking. Products are classified and ranked, not sequenced.

INFERRED explanation: the platform's genealogy is a *directory/registry* business (2019 manufacturer directory → 2020 cloud → 2024 data products), so its data model grew from entity enumeration rather than from technology progression. It models *who and where*, not *what generation*.

## 9) Historical technology activity display

- **月度更新** cadence published (E6) — a maintenance commitment.
- Databases include 历史 records implicitly through registration and approval history, and 产业宏观 (industry macro) suggests long-run series.
- **No coverage start date, no timeline feature, and no as-of/versioning model is published.** Given the company was founded 2017 and platform built 2019, effective historical depth is **likely ~2019 onward** — INFERRED from company history, not stated.
- The published analyses (e.g. 颅内介入取栓器械, IVUS/OCT 国产替代 pieces) do perform multi-year retrospectives, implying retrospective capability exists.

## 10) Prediction capability: marketed vs validated

**Marketed: essentially none at the predictive level.** The strongest language is 前瞻/趋势 and the existence of 市场排名分析模型 and 价格区间分析模型 — these are *descriptive models*, explicitly called 分析模型 (analysis models), not forecast models. MDRADAR uses "NLP等AI手段" for **lead mining** (商机挖掘), not prediction.

**Validated: nothing.** No accuracy figure, no backtest, no evaluation, no benchmark — and no predictive claim to attach one to.

**Verdict:** **no prediction claim, therefore no unsubstantiated prediction marketing, and no validation evidence.** The AI usage is explicitly scoped to cleaning (智能清洗), dedup, and lead extraction (E16, E17) — a defensible, narrow application. Recorded as: predictive capability **not claimed**.

## 11) What MedRadar can learn conceptually

1. **Dual taxonomy architecture** — a 3-level product/regulatory hierarchy **plus** a 4-level market-track hierarchy, kept as separate dimensions. This is the cleanest answer in the audit to "how do I classify both by technology and by market," and it directly informs MedRadar's design.
2. **A product-classification database as the join hub** (E7) — every other database links through 产品分类库. That is a concrete, copyable architectural choice (concept, not code).
3. **Role-separated organisation databases** — manufacturer / distributor / contract-manufacturer / agent as distinct entities rather than one "company" table.
4. **Customs HS-code linkage** (E10) — a trade dimension that turns device products into import/export time series. Novel and cheap for MedRadar to add conceptually.
5. **Exchange-listed data products with four-dimension compliance assessment** (E11) — a real, externally-validated template for how to publish provenance and licensing for a device dataset. MedRadar can mirror the four dimensions (entity, source, tradability, circulation risk) in its own data card.
6. **The buyer/采购单位 as a first-class entity** — reframes competitor analysis to include demand.
7. **Import-substitution (进口替代) as a first-class analytic** — the dominant competitive narrative in Chinese medtech; MedRadar should model domestic/import origin explicitly.
8. **Splitting B2B data from tooling** (databases vs MDBIDS/MDRADAR apps) — a licensable-data-plus-application revenue shape.

## 12) What must NOT be copied

1. **A JS-only public web presence with no server-rendered content.** The site is unreadable to crawlers, auditors, and any client without full JS execution — and it was blocked entirely in a real browser. This is an auditability failure MedRadar must invert.
2. **CAPTCHA-walled corporate communications** — the vendor's own product documentation sits behind WeChat CAPTCHA, so third parties cannot read the spec.
3. **Counts as capability** — "500余万条" and "50多个数据库" communicate volume, not fitness for a question.
4. **No corporate-hierarchy ER.** A registry-derived platform that never publishes subsidiary/M&A handling leaves the hardest identity problem unsolved.
5. **Distribution through syndicated PR rather than documentation.** All structural knowledge here came from self-published portal articles; a product should document itself.
6. **Data-exchange listing alone as a quality signal** — compliance assessment covers legality and circulation risk, **not accuracy or completeness**. Do not treat "listed on an exchange" as validation of data quality.

## 13) Decision + reasoning

**Decision: STUDY_ONLY.** (Primary website itself: **BLOCKED**; the audit rests on the vendor's own syndicated publications and exchange coverage.)

Reasoning: ADOPT/ADAPT impossible — closed subscription, no public API, no schema access, site unreachable for direct inspection. Study value is **high on taxonomy architecture** (3-level product + 4-level track dual taxonomy; classification-DB-as-hub), **high on provenance governance** (four-dimension exchange compliance assessment is the only externally-reviewed provenance model found), and **high on domain modelling** (role-separated organisations, buyer as entity, customs linkage, import substitution). It is simultaneously the **worst case study in public accessibility** in the set, which is itself a design lesson for MedRadar.
