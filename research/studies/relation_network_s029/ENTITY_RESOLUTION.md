## 7. Verdict

The identity layer is usable for a temporal study only with the documented caveats: 53.8% of 6940 company strings resolve to a company, but 2.31% of accepted mappings have an UNKNOWN ownership date and 0.00% are measured anachronisms. Claims resting on the UNKNOWN entities must be excluded from any time-sensitive test rather than silently accepted.

Numbers behind the verdict (all in the JSON artifact): claim-producing rate 53.8% (3730/6940 mentions), ownership-date-unknown share 2.31%, valid_from anachronism share 0.00%, rejected mentions 73.

## 8. Method and limitations

- The measurement population is the per-string mention record `data/company_mentions.jsonl`, which is the input of the claim builder; when `data/claims.jsonl` exists the claim-level linkage is added without altering these numbers.
- `claim-producing` follows the pipeline's accepted statuses; the tier table is independent.
- Ownership dates are used as the register states them. Where the register says UNKNOWN, the report says UNKNOWN and the mapping is downgraded (section 3.3), never back-filled with a plausible date.
- Recall is measured on ClinicalTrials.gov discovery queries only; Europe PMC affiliations are measured through the mention records in sections 1-4.
