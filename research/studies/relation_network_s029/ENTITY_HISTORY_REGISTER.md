## 0. What this register is for

S-029 measures relations between five medical-device companies using trial sponsorships, publication affiliations and awards. Every one of those sources carries a *string*, never a guaranteed legal entity. This register fixes, for each company, which legal persons existed when, under which names, and which observed strings are companies at all. It exists so that a 2026 group structure cannot be projected backwards onto earlier periods: e.g. 'Covidien' before 2015-01-26 is not Medtronic; 'Mindray Medical International Limited' is not the SZSE-listed Shenzhen company; 'Draeger' is often a German surname.

## 2. Rules used

### 2.1 Role-based validity

`valid_from` / `valid_to` delimit the period in which the entity existed **in the role named by `entity_type`**, not necessarily the lifetime of the legal person. `medtronic_inc` is a `former_parent` ending 2015-01-26; the legal person 'Medtronic, Inc.' still exists as a Minnesota subsidiary (FY2025 Exhibit 21 lists it). `covidien_plc` is likewise a `former_parent`: the independent Irish parent 2009-06-04 → 2015-01-26, after which it was acquired and is absent from Medtronic's FY2025 Exhibit 21. Anachronism testing uses `valid_from` only, so a claim dated before a company's `valid_from` is a claim about an entity that did not yet exist / a subsidiary the group did not yet own.

### 2.2 Confidence rule

identity_confidence is assigned by rule, not per-case taste: 0.95 = canonical identity confirmed by >=2 independent official documents AND a resolved stable registry id; 0.90 = one official document plus a resolved stable registry id, or >=2 independent official documents without an id; 0.80 = one official document or one registry record that names the entity and its role but no second source; 0.60 = the entity is attested only by corpus/observed strings with parentage inferred from observed naming context (explicitly flagged as inferred); 0.40 = the string is attested but the underlying legal entity could not be pinned down.

### 2.3 Operating-unit rule (stated once)

Rule (stated once, not re-derived per string): every string of the form 'Medtronic <unit>' (e.g. 'Medtronic - MITG', 'Medtronic Cardiac Rhythm and Heart Failure', 'Medtronic BRC') is an operating unit or department of the Medtronic group, not a separate legal entity; it maps to the group parent medtronic_plc unless the string names one of the registered subsidiaries. Rationale: the FY2025 Exhibit 21 lists the group's legal entities; a few observed strings do match legal persons (Medtronic MiniMed, Inc., Medtronic Xomed, Inc., and 'Medtronic Vascular' which is the prefix of Medtronic Vascular, Inc.) and are registered as subsidiaries or as aliases of them, while the remaining 'Medtronic <unit>' strings (MITG, Cardiac Rhythm and Heart Failure, BRC, Spinal and Biologics, ...) are divisional/portfolio names used in trial sponsorship. The same rule is applied to 'GE Healthcare <place>' (site/affiliate), 'Philips Healthcare <place>' and 'Shenzhen Mindray Bio-Medical Electronics Co., Ltd.' (registered parent).

### 2.4 Brand ≠ legal entity, former ≠ current

Brands and divisions are typed `brand` / `division` and never carry a SEC CIK or LEI unless the same string is also a registered legal person. Conversely, `former_subsidiary` and `former_parent` are used for entities that were once inside (or at the head of) the group but are not now: Covidien plc (independent public parent 2009-06-04 → acquired 2015-01-26; no evidence it survives as a legal person), Covidien Ltd. (parent 2007 → 2009), Datascope Corp. (never a Mindray entity — it was the *seller* of the patient-monitoring business), MiniMed Inc. (acquired 2001), Philips Lighting N.V. (now Signify N.V.), Dräger Medical GmbH (LEI status INACTIVE, successor is the KGaA).

## 5. Mapping rules the pipeline must apply

These rules follow directly from the dates and types above; they are the operational content of the register.

1. **Covidien before 2015-01-26 is not Medtronic.** A claim dated before 2015-01-26 whose only company string is 'Covidien' / 'Covidien plc' maps to `covidien_plc`, an independent public parent with `parent_entity_key = null`. Only from 2015-01-26 does Covidien activity belong to the Medtronic group. Covidien-branded *legal entities* that survive inside Medtronic (Covidien AG, Covidien Limited, ...) are listed as subsidiaries of `medtronic_plc` and are valid after 2015-01-26.
2. **'Tyco Healthcare' is neither Covidien nor Medtronic.** It maps to `tyco_healthcare` (`division`), valid up to 2007-06-29. A pre-2007 claim naming it is a claim about Tyco International's healthcare business.
3. **'Mindray Medical International Limited' is not the SZSE issuer.** It maps to `mindray_medical_international_ltd` (`former_parent`, Cayman, NYSE ADS 2006-09-26 → private 2016-03-03). The ChiNext-listed company (300760, listed 2018-10-16) is `shenzhen_mindray`. Any claim that uses the Cayman issuer's name as if it were the 2018+ listed company is a name collision, not a relation.
4. **'Datascope Corp.' has two periods.** Before 2008-05-14 it is the independent seller `datascope_corp`; the *business* it sold is `datascope_patient_monitoring_business`, inside Mindray from 2008-05-14. Datascope Corp. itself was never acquired by Mindray. A measured affiliation string places it later under Getinge; the Getinge date is unverified, so no date is asserted.
5. **Bare 'Draeger' is ambiguous.** It is both the company's ASCII transliteration and a frequent German surname (author names, e-mail local parts). It must not be mapped to the company without a corroborating attribute (affiliation with Lübeck, a draeger.com address, a legal-form token, or a registered alias). The not_company entries above give the measured counter-examples.
6. **Divisions and brands carry no legal-entity identifiers.** 'Philips Healthcare' (from 2008-01-01), 'Philips Medical Systems' (≤ 2008-01-01), 'GE Healthcare', 'GE Medical Systems' and 'Medtronic <unit>' are divisions/sites of the parent; a claim naming them maps to the parent company only through their `division`/`brand` entities, and inherits no CIK/LEI from them.
7. **Anachronism test.** For every claim, map the company string, then compare the claim's `declared_at`/`published_at` with the mapped entity's `valid_from`. A date before `valid_from` means the claim attributes activity to an entity that did not yet exist or to a subsidiary the group did not yet own (e.g. a 2012 'Covidien' claim tagged as Medtronic).

## 6. Recorded conflicts and UNKNOWNs

### 6.1 Conflicts between sources (not resolved silently)

1. **Acquirer naming in the Covidien transaction.** The 2014-07-17 Medtronic, Inc. 8-K describes 'the acquisition of Covidien ... by Medtronic, Inc.'; the FY2015 10-K and the 2015-01-26 press release describe Medtronic plc as the acquirer. Both are correct for their own entity: the transaction was effected by an Irish scheme plus a merger of the merger subsidiary into Medtronic, Inc., so the acquirer's legal identity changed *during* the closing. The register therefore does not assert a single acquirer entity for the pre-closing period.
2. **Transaction value.** Completion press release: 'approximately $49.9 billion'; FY2015 10-K: 'approximately $50 billion'. The register records the completion fact, not the valuation.
3. **Mindray 'relisting 2018' framing.** The study brief says the Cayman issuer was 'delisted 2016, relisted 2018'. No relisting of Mindray Medical International Limited occurred: it went private on 2016-03-03 and remains a private Cayman entity (GLEIF status ACTIVE). The 2018-10-16 event is the ChiNext IPO of a *different* legal person, Shenzhen Mindray Bio-Medical Electronics Co., Ltd. Treating the two as one issuer is a name-collision case.
4. **Datascope acquisition date.** Mindray's press release is dated 2008-05-15 and says 'today announced the completion'; the seller's 8-K states the sale closed 2008-05-14 (effective 2008-05-01). The register uses 2008-05-14 as the closing date and records the announcement date.
5. **Dräger registry creation date.** GLEIF reports `creationDate` 1970-06-29 for Drägerwerk AG & Co. KGaA, while the corporate group is normally dated to 1889. The legal meaning of the 1970 date (formation vs. conversion into the current form) is not verified, so `valid_from` stays UNKNOWN and the 1970 date is kept only in the provenance record.
6. **Philips Healthcare start.** The FY2008 20-F calls it 'our new Healthcare sector' with effect from 2008-01-01. Any earlier use of 'Philips Healthcare' as a *sector* name is not attested in the fetched filings: before 2008 the corresponding division is 'Philips Medical Systems' (attested as the 'former Medical Systems division' and, by a 2006 third-party 10-K, as a supplier name), so the sector reorganisation is dated to 2008-01-01 and the older string is a separate division entity.
