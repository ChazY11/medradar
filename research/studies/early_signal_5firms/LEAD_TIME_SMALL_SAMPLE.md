# NINE-EVENT LEAD-TIME DESCRIPTION (S1-crosswalk-effective)

**descriptive only; n = 9 independent event(s), below the floor of 10, therefore underpowered and not a lead-time estimate.**

Source pairs: 17 → distinct later clearances: **9**. Representative = latest paired trial per event (minimum lead); the maximum is reported only as an upper bound.

## Distribution of representative leads (quarters)

- median **3.3**, IQR [2.26, 9.31], range [2.09, 19.67]
- share with lead ≥ 1 quarter: **1.0** (see the conditioning note: this is not a finding)
- share with lead ≥ 2 quarters: **1.0** — **a tautology**, every event was admitted only because its lead was already ≥ 2 quarters

**Conditioning caveat.** The distribution is truncated at 2 quarters by the G1 eligibility rule, so the median describes a selected sample and the shares are artefacts of selection. It says nothing about how often leads are shorter than two quarters.
