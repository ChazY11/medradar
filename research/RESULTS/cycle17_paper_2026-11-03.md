## Why this cycle

The mission objective lists **papers** as deliverables and seventeen cycles had produced a large,
consistent record without a synthesis. The material is unusually suitable for a methods-and-negative-
results paper: five retractions with mechanisms, a controlled-truth frontier, a calibration result, a
rolling-origin null, and a point-in-time chain with an audited leakage fix.

## What the paper says

- **Question.** Can corporate technological evolution be anticipated from public regulatory filings?
- **Corpus.** 31,802 premarket submissions, 2016-11 … 2026-09 (119 months), 9,412 deterministic legal
  entities, 38 tracks; plus 2,663 device trial events (43 tracks, 46 companies) and a 13-firm archival
  hiring lane.
- **Results.** Best detector sensitivity 0.223 at 2 % FAR; detector ranking not identifiable above a
  2-month bin; five retracted findings, each with the control that killed it; a ratio rule flags 35 of 53
  companies where an exact binomial test supports 4; a rolling-origin null where no interval excludes 1
  and the calibrated signal fires 5 times in 717 observations; 40 stable / 13 changed / 2 reversed
  verdicts across four as-of dates; a point-in-time rebuild with 0 leakage problems after closing a real
  leak.
- **Contribution.** Not a predictive success but an instrument: a detection frontier with calibrated
  false-alarm rates, a momentum test with mandatory minimum detectable effects, a point-in-time profile
  chain with hash manifests, and seven measurement rules that each came from a destroyed claim.

## The sourcing rule

Every number is declared in `papers/CLAIMS.json` with its artifact and exact substring, and
`papers/verify_claims.py` checks both directions — the artifact must contain the value and the paper must
contain it too. **45 of 45 claims verified.**

Drafting failed this check three times before passing: two figures were mis-transcribed from memory and
one result was omitted from the text. That is precisely the failure mode the check exists to catch, and it
caught the author, not a reader.
