## The structural finding

MISSION.md mandates five deep-case companies: **Medtronic, GE HealthCare, Philips, Dräger, Mindray**.
Under the unchanged usability floor, the hiring lane covers **one** of them (Medtronic). The reasons
differ and are not fixable by more probing:

- **GE HealthCare** became an independent filer only in 2023 — a historical hiring series predating
  its independence does not exist.
- **Philips** has a genuine board but covers 38 of 119 months: archived too sparsely for a monthly
  series that passes the silence criterion.
- **Dräger and Mindray** post on systems whose job-level URLs are not archived; only marketing pages
  were captured.
- **Masimo** returned nothing.

## Consequence

The hiring lane can serve the *statistical* question (3 usable companies) but **cannot** serve the
mandated deep-case requirement. Those five companies need evidence types that actually exist for
them: their regulatory filings (held), device trials (held for 46 firms), and publications.

**Deliberately not done:** the floor was not loosened for Philips, and the silence criterion was not
relaxed to manufacture a fourth company. Probing stopped because the marginal firm is unreachable from
this source — continuing would be the specification search the mission forbids.
