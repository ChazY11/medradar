## What the audit can and cannot do

It is a **completeness and reproducibility** check: it proves artifacts exist, are committed, and still verify.
It **cannot** prove the science is right — no script can. The retraction discipline and the claim maps are what
stand in for that; the audit only refuses to let a missing or broken artifact survive into a later round.
