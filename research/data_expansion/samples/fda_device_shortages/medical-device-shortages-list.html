Update: October 26, 2026

The FDA is updating the medical device shortage list (MDSL) by removing angiographic control syringes with rotating adapters (product code DXT), and adding implantable gastric electrical stimulation systems (product code LNQ) and discontinuances reported since June 2026.

The FDA publishes a device shortage list to provide transparency to the public, particularly those who use or purchase medical devices. Section 506J of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FD&C Act) requires the FDA to maintain a publicly available, up-to-date list of the devices determined to be in shortage. The medical device shortage list reflects the categories of devices the FDA has determined to be in shortage. The presence of a device type on this list does not necessarily indicate that patient care has been affected. In addition, the FDA provides a list of medical devices for which the FDA has been notified that manufacturing has been permanently discontinued.

The Office of Supply Chain Resilience is responsible for managing CDRH's activities to anticipate and prevent disruptions to the supply chain for medical devices, including managing the medical Device Shortage List and the Discontinuance List.
