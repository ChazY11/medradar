## Conclusion

  contact; there is no public price list and every non-marketing route 404s. Access is a
  negotiated subscription, and the platform is an analyst web application with optional
  data feeds under contract.

  shipment and customs records, company overviews and contact information on over 10 million

## Field inventory advertised
- Bill-of-lading and customs records (US, and a set of other countries), consignee/shipper
  names and addresses, product descriptions, HS codes, weights, TEU, carriers, dates;
  corporate hierarchy linkage (S&P entity IDs) — the hierarchy is the differentiator vs
  ImportGenius/Datamyne.

## Verdict
Paid + contract-negotiated; no price to quote from a public page (deliberately). Only worth
  pursuing if MedRadar needs cross-country firm-level flows with corporate hierarchy, and only
  with a real budget and a licence review.
