UN Comtrade Database

Every product has a code. Every trade has a record.

The world's most comprehensive trade data platform: detailed annual and monthly imports and exports by product and trading partner, reported by about 200 countries and areas to the United Nations and classified the same way so they can be compared.

Explore it here, download it, or connect your own applications through the API. Premium subscribers get bulk files and higher limits.

How the data reaches you

The figures come from national authorities, not estimates. Knowing the path helps explain revisions and gaps.

  1. 1

    Countries report

    National statistical offices and customs authorities send their annual and monthly trade figures to the United Nations.

  2. 2

    The Statistics Division standardizes

    The data is checked, converted to US dollars and to common classifications, so countries can be compared.

  3. 3

    You use it

    It is published openly here, in bulk files and through the API, and revised when countries send corrections.
